HOW TO UPDATE
1. Open human-status.txt on GitHub and click the pencil (Edit).
2. Refresh before editing. Change only your own section.
3. Write short, plain sentences. Include your update time and time zone.
4. Use one status: Ready, Working, Need help, Waiting, or Done.
5. Preview and commit with your name, e.g. "gretsky: update human status".
6. Update after an inject, a completed task, a blocker, or a change of task.
7. When handing work off, name the person and link the finding or draft.
8. Never fill in another person's progress without their report.
   AI helpers may draft an update, but must label it as a draft until you confirm.

Blank or "Not reported" means no personal update was recorded.
It does not mean the person has done no work.
